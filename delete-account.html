Delete Your HomeQuestConnect Account

This page explains how to permanently delete your HomeQuestConnect account and what happens to your data when you do.

1 Delete from within the app (recommended)

  1. Open the HomeQuestConnect app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Account tab.
  3. Scroll to the Danger Zone section and tap Delete My Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion, then type DELETE to confirm a second time.
This action is immediate and cannot be undone. Your account is permanently deactivated as soon as you confirm.

2 Request deletion by email

If you can't access the app, email support@homequest.co.zw from the email address associated with your account, with the subject line "Delete my account". We'll process your request and confirm once it's complete, typically within a few business days.

What gets deleted, and what's retained

DataWhat happens
Name, email, phone numberPermanently removed / anonymized immediately
Login credentialsPermanently deleted immediately — you can never sign back into this account
Property listings you postedPermanently deleted, including all photos
Saved/wishlisted propertiesPermanently deleted
NotificationsPermanently deleted
Payment / subscription recordsRetained, disassociated from your name/email, as required for financial recordkeeping

Once your account is deleted, you're free to create a new HomeQuestConnect account using the same email address if you choose to come back.

Questions about this process? Contact us at support@homequest.co.zw. See also our Privacy Policy.
